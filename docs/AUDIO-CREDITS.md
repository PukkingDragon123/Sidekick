## How licenses were verified

All checks were made on **2026-11-10** from the build environment, by downloading each source page and each archive and reading them directly. Nothing below relies on a search-result filter or on a third-party summary.

### OpenGameArt.org submissions (CC0)

- For every submission used, the submission page itself was downloaded and its **`License(s)`** field was read. A source was accepted only when that field lists **CC0 and nothing else**, with the label linking to `http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`. Submissions offering several licenses, or CC-BY / OGA-BY / CC-BY-SA / GPL, were not used.
- The exact field found on each page (label and link), the page's own "Copyright/Attribution Notice", the posting date, and sha256 hashes of the page copy and of the downloaded source files are recorded in `web/public/sfx/licenses/source-license-evidence.json`. The generator of this document refuses to run if any of those pages lists a license other than CC0.
- Where an archive ships its own license file, that file was read too and is copied verbatim into `web/public/sfx/licenses/`:
  - **Kenney** – *Interface Sounds* `License.txt`: "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) … This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects. Support us by crediting Kenney or www.kenney.nl (this is not mandatory)". *RPG Audio* and *Music Jingles* `license.txt`: "License (CC0) … You may use these graphics in personal and commercial projects. Credit (Kenney or www.kenney.nl) would be nice but is not mandatory."
  - **Vehicle** – *Tinysized SFX* `readme.txt`: "Distributed under Creative Commons Zero".
  - **qubodup** – *Squeaky Rat* `notes.txt`: "released under CC0. The sounds used to create these were under CC0 at the time of creation."
- The Kenney packs were taken from Kenney's own uploads on OpenGameArt (author account "Kenney"), because `kenney.nl` answered HTTP 403 to this environment. They are the same packs, with Kenney's license file inside each zip.

### U.S. National Park Service recordings (public domain)

- Four recordings (`amb_wind.mp3`, `owl_hoot.mp3`, `bee_buzz.mp3`, `frog_croak.mp3`) come from nps.gov. They are **public domain, not CC0**.
- Yellowstone Sound Library page (`https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/soundlibrary.htm`) states: "The files available here were recorded in the park and are in the public domain. They may be downloaded and used without limitation; however, please credit the "National Park Service" where appropriate."
- NPS Natural Sounds – Sound Gallery page (`https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm`) states: "The files are in the public domain and may be downloaded. Enjoy, and please give the appropriate credit."
- Each recording's own page was read for its credit line (wind: "NPS/Peter Comley"; owl, bee, pig frog: "NPS"). The statements and page entries are quoted in `web/public/sfx/licenses/nps_*_public-domain-statement.txt`.
- **Requested credit:** "National Park Service". It is a courtesy request, not a license condition; the suggested credits line above includes it.

### What could not be checked

- `kenney.nl`, `freesound.org` and `cdn.freesound.org` returned HTTP 403 from the sites themselves, so no file was taken from them and no page on them was read.
- Two OpenGameArt submissions say they are derived from Freesound uploads: *Fantozzi's Footsteps* (page text: taken from Fantozzi's Freesound pack 10338 "under CC0") and *Squeaky Rat* (`notes.txt`: based on Freesound sounds that "were under CC0 at the time of creation"). The CC0 grant read here is the one on the OpenGameArt page / in the archive; the original Freesound pages could not be re-checked.
- *UI Sound Effects* by Robin Lamb says its sounds come from the public-domain sample libraries VCSL and VSCO 2 CE; those libraries' own pages were not read. The CC0 grant read is the OpenGameArt one.

### Origin of the sounds

- Nothing in this set was generated with an AI model or synthesised in code by this project.
- The sources are a mix of field/foley recordings (footsteps, rustles, water, crunches, birds, stream, fire, crickets, owl, bee, frog, cat, coins, paper) and human-made sound design with instruments or synthesizers (Kenney's interface tones and jingles, the chimes, the win jingle, the magic and power-up sounds), as described by their authors on the source pages.
- Whether an uploader used generative tools cannot be proven from a web page. Of the 32 OpenGameArt submissions used, 27 were posted between 2010 and 2022. The five newer ones are *Chimey UI Sounds* (2024, "I decided to make my own"), *Kalimba Two-Notes* (2024, "two notes played on a kalimba"), *Various Sound Effects* (2024, "scraps from various old unfinished projects") and *Power-Up Sound Effects* (2024) by Spring Spring, and *Elevator ding* (2025, "made using some synth in Garage Band"). The four NPS files are field recordings.

## Notes for maintainers

- Sounds were chosen by reading file names and descriptions and by measuring duration, envelope, pitch contour and spectrum. They were **not auditioned by ear**, so a listening pass is worth doing; `manifest.json` gains are suggestions derived from measured loudness.
- One-shots: mono, 44.1 kHz, 112 kbps MP3, leading silence trimmed, peak about -3 dBFS (`ui_type` and `voice_*` about -8 dBFS). Loops: stereo, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps MP3, about -20 LUFS, 0.5 s fade in and out (the app is expected to crossfade).
- `pet_purr.mp3` is a real cat purr with almost all of its energy below 300 Hz; it is quiet on phone speakers.
- `amb_night.mp3` is an 11.5 s seamless cricket loop repeated three times; `amb_birds.mp3` contains natural pauses between calls.
- Some slots reuse a pack sound under a different role than its file name suggests (for example `ui_error` is Kenney's `question_004`, `plant_dig` is a gravel step, `voice_d` is a short rat squeak). The table above lists every original file.
